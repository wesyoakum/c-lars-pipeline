# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-05-20

1.0 marks Pipeline as the system of record for new business — all new opportunities, quotes, OCs, and jobs are created here. WorkflowMax is now archive-only and the historical import continues toward full decommission. Subsequent 1.x releases will complete the WFM data migration, finish the full quote lifecycle, and harden multi-user onboarding.
